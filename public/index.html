Price Basis: Shelf Price vs. Net Paid
Shelf Price (Pre-Discount):

Uses regular sticker prices before manufacturer rebates. Best for seeing pure product inflation without the noise of temporary promotional sales.

Net Paid (Post-Discount):

Uses what you actually paid after all Costco Instant Savings coupons. Best for seeing the real out-of-pocket cash impact on your grocery bills.
